Arduino UNO for Windows 8 64位元 driver

荒廢了幾個月的arduino﹐最近趁著剛研究完WCF 再來重新復習一下。

前幾天從露天買了些套件回來﹐將Arduino UNO R3 接上USB 並指定了driver路徑後﹐在windows 8 之下安裝driver 竟然出現了錯誤﹐驚~~~。

[![5_沒有簽章_安裝失敗](images/90402-01.png "5_沒有簽章_安裝失敗")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/5_%E6%B2%92%E6%9C%89%E7%B0%BD%E7%AB%A0_%E5%AE%89%E8%A3%9D%E5%A4%B1%E6%95%97_2.png)

錯誤是因為沒有簽章而無法安裝﹐上網查了一下﹐這個在windows 8 32 位元下不會有這個問題﹐在64位元之下會﹐不過不是沒有解決的辦法。

1.將滑鼠移到右上或右下角﹐出現WIN 8特有的功能列﹐以滑鼠點選最下方的【設定】。

[![設定](images/90402-02.jpg "設定")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/%E8%A8%AD%E5%AE%9A_2.jpg)

2.右方的功能列變更為【設定】的選項﹐再點選最下方的【變更電腦設定】。

[![設定2](images/90402-03.jpg "設定2")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/%E8%A8%AD%E5%AE%9A2_2.jpg)

3.畫面切換為【電腦設定】﹐先點選左方的【一般】﹐再點選右方畫面最下方的【立即重新啟動】。

[![電腦設定](images/90402-04.jpg "電腦設定")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/%E9%9B%BB%E8%85%A6%E8%A8%AD%E5%AE%9A_2.jpg)

4.畫面會先路一陣子﹐再出現【選擇選項】後﹐以滑鼠點選【疑難排解】﹐然後再點選【進階選項】。

[![IMG_20130207_225119](images/90402-05.jpg "IMG_20130207_225119")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/IMG_20130207_225119_2.jpg)

5.在【進階選項】中再點選【啟動設定】

[![IMG_20130207_225445](images/90402-06.jpg "IMG_20130207_225445")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/IMG_20130207_225445_2.jpg)

6.在啟動設定畫面﹐直接按下【重新啟動】

[![IMG_20130207_225458](images/90402-07.jpg "IMG_20130207_225458")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/IMG_20130207_225458_2.jpg)

7.接著電腦會重新開機﹐畫面會出現【啟動設定】﹐在這按下數字鍵 **7** **停用驅動程式強制簽章**

[![IMG_20130207_225533](images/90402-08.jpg "IMG_20130207_225533")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/IMG_20130207_225533_2.jpg)

8.電腦重新開後﹐就可以再連接Arduino UNO 安裝驅動程式。安裝過程會出現「Windows 無法驗證此驅動程式軟體的發行者」﹐點擊「仍然安裝此驅動程式軟體」即可安裝好驅動程式。

[![11_安裝](images/90402-09.png "11_安裝")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/11_%E5%AE%89%E8%A3%9D_2.png)

[![11_安裝完成](images/90402-10.png "11_安裝完成")](https://dotblogsfile.blob.core.windows.net/user/nethawk/1302/Arduino-UNO-for-Windows-8-64-driver_CA53/11_%E5%AE%89%E8%A3%9D%E5%AE%8C%E6%88%90_2.png)
